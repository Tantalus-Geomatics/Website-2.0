**What our survey provides.** A consolidation survey in Squamish joins adjoining surveyed lots into one parcel. The District's [subdivision page](https://squamish.ca/building-and-land-development/home-land-and-property-development/land-development-applications/subdivision/) treats consolidation as a subdivision. The registrar may accept a reference plan or an explanatory plan, without a description, for that new parcel. The fee to consolidate two lots, or to move the line between two lots, is $500 under [Fees and Charges Bylaw No. 3185](https://squamish.civicweb.net/document/259432).

**Servicing and parkland.** For a single-unit or two-unit residential consolidation or lot line adjustment that does not create a new buildable lot, the [Subdivision and Development Control Bylaw](https://squamish.civicweb.net/document/200228) does not require works and services. Park dedication does not apply to a consolidation of existing parcels. Those are two different rules.

**The application.** The owner signs the application, and each holder of a registered charge consents. A mortgagee's consent extends that mortgage over the new parcel.
