**What our survey provides.** A proposed strata plan in Squamish shows the intended strata lots for the design and for a disclosure statement. It does not create title. Title arises when the strata plan is deposited at the Land Title Office.

**Unit entitlement.** For a residential strata lot that is not bare land, the Strata Property Act allows three bases: the habitable area measured by a BC land surveyor, the same whole number for every lot, or a number the superintendent approves.

**Squamish limits.** In the R-1 and R-4 zones, [Zoning Bylaw No. 2200, 2011](https://squamish.civicweb.net/document/269651) allows no more than four strata-titled dwelling units on a lot. A secondary suite or an accessory dwelling unit cannot be strata-titled.
