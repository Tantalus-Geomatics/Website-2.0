**What our survey provides.** A proposed strata plan in Whistler shows the intended strata lots for the design. It does not create title. Title arises when the strata plan is deposited at the Land Title Office.

**A new building.** If the strata plan includes a building that has not been previously occupied, a British Columbia land surveyor endorses the plan, certifying that the building has not been previously occupied. The endorsement is dated no more than 180 days before the plan is tendered for deposit. That endorsement is the surveyor's. The Strata Property Act does not assign it to the Surveyor General.

**A conversion.** A strata plan that includes a previously occupied building is submitted to Municipal Council. Council may approve it, approve it with terms, or refuse it. The survey for that application is on the [strata surveys](/whistler/services/strata-surveys/) page.
