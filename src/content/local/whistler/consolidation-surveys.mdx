**What our survey provides.** A consolidation survey in Whistler joins adjoining lots into one parcel. It is a land title plan, not a separate Resort Municipality consolidation permit. Your lawyer or notary deposits the plan at the Land Title Office.

**The Resort Municipality.** Zoning, a development permit, or the Approving Officer can still apply if the plan creates new boundaries or dedications. The [Official Community Plan](https://www.whistler.ca/ocp/) says development and subdivision in a development permit area need a development permit, unless exempted. A subdivision of a building under the Strata Property Act is excepted from that sentence. Detached and duplex lots are often exempt, except where any part of the lot is in an environmental development permit area and within 30 metres of the high water mark of a stream, or in the RI1 zone.

**Usable area.** Where the new parcel is later built on, [Zoning and Parking Bylaw No. 303, 2015](https://www.whistler.ca/wp-content/uploads/Zoning-_and_Parking_Bylaw_303_2015_2026-08-07.pdf) still measures usable site area by leaving out water, land within 15 metres of a watercourse, and the steeper ground the zone excludes.
