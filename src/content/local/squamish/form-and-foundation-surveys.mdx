**What our survey provides.** A form or foundation survey in Squamish shows where the forms or the concrete sit relative to the lot lines. Locating a form or a foundation against a property line, in order to certify that location in writing, is work a practising BC land surveyor does.

**The District.** [Building Bylaw No. 1822, 2004](https://squamish.civicweb.net/document/158787) includes a survey plan prepared by a British Columbia land surveyor with the building permit application. The building official may, in writing, require an up-to-date survey that shows the proposed top of concrete before and after the foundations and footings are poured. Once that written requirement is served, it has to be met. The District's inspection list asks for a form survey before the footing and foundation inspection, to confirm the forms are within the setbacks. [Zoning Bylaw No. 2200, 2011](https://squamish.civicweb.net/document/269651) says no building or structure, other than a sign, may sit within 4.57 metres of the Highway 99 right of way.

**Elevations.** Tantalus does this work in CGVD28 unless you explicitly ask for another datum. We will adopt CGVD2013 only once it is the official vertical datum in use in British Columbia. In the floodplain, the building inspector may require a BC land surveyor's certificate for the flood construction level and the setback. The District's flood construction level maps are CGVD28.
