**What our survey provides.** A strata survey in Squamish defines the strata lots and the common property. For a new building, a BC land surveyor certifies that the building has not been previously occupied. That endorsement is dated no more than 180 days before the plan is tendered for deposit.

**The District.** A conversion of a previously occupied building is considered by [Council](https://squamish.ca/building-and-land-development/home-land-and-property-development/land-development-applications/strata-conversion/). For a small building, [Land Development Procedures Bylaw No. 2632](https://squamish.civicweb.net/document/195835) lets the Approving Officer decide: a commercial building of five units or fewer, or a residential building of up to two dwellings. Bare land strata needs an approving officer's approval before the plan is deposited, and the lots have to meet the zone's minimum lot size. The fee to subdivide bare land strata is $1,000 a lot, with a $3,000 minimum. A strata conversion fee is $1,000 a lot, with a $1,000 minimum, under [Fees and Charges Bylaw No. 3185](https://squamish.civicweb.net/document/259432).

**Phased strata and amendments.** An approving officer approves the phased strata plan declaration. That approval expires after one year unless the first phase is deposited. A schedule of voting rights is filed only if voting rights are set out in a schedule. Consolidating strata lots, and designating limited common property, each need a unanimous vote.
